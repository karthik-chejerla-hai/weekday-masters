# Money page disclosure

## Requirements
- Keep one persistent header balance and profile control, plus the YTD Analytics link.
- Open the member top-up form only on request. Keep validation, failure feedback and balance refresh.
- Keep asset values, shuttle counts and warnings visible. Put reconciliation and audit dates in accessible details.
- Place court top-up, shuttle purchase and bank audit actions below their matching cards in one desktop row. Use one shared form area. Cancel or successful save closes the area and returns focus.
- Bank audit uses the existing correction review and audit history, with Bank selected initially.
- Preserve permissions, accounting, warning thresholds and the original member RSVP pills.
